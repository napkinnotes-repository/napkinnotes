# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: October 2026**

Napkin Notes is a scientific outreach publication available at [napkinnotes.es](https://napkinnotes.es) and through the Napkin Notes Android application.

The Android application displays content from napkinnotes.es using an Android WebView.

## Data collection

Napkin Notes does not require users to create an account and does not request access to contacts, precise location, camera, microphone, files, SMS, phone data, or other sensitive device permissions.

Napkin Notes uses **Cloudflare Web Analytics** to obtain aggregated usage statistics such as:

- page views,
- visits,
- country,
- browser,
- device type,
- operating system.

Cloudflare Web Analytics is designed as a privacy-focused analytics service and does not use cookies for this purpose.

The analytics information is used only to understand general traffic patterns and improve the website and application.

## Article comments and Formspree

Visitors may choose to submit a comment or reply using the forms on our articles. These forms request a name and the comment text, and allow visitors to provide an optional email address. They also send the article title and URL, and, for replies, information identifying the comment being answered.

Comment submissions are sent to **Formspree**, an external service that processes form submissions and delivers them to the Napkin Notes editorial team. Formspree may process technical information associated with these requests under its own [Privacy Policy](https://formspree.io/legal/privacy-policy/).

All comments and replies are reviewed before publication. If a submission is approved, the name supplied and the comment or reply text may be displayed publicly on Napkin Notes. **Email addresses are not published.** An email address provided voluntarily may be used to contact the submitter about their submission.

To ask about a submitted or published comment, visitors can contact us using the address below.

## External links

Napkin Notes articles may contain links to external websites, scientific publications, repositories, institutions, or other third-party services.

These external websites operate independently and may have their own privacy policies and data-processing practices. Napkin Notes is not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party websites.

## Personal information

Napkin Notes does not require users to provide personal information in order to read articles or use the Android application.

If users voluntarily contact Napkin Notes by email, the information they provide is used to respond to their message. Information submitted through article comment forms is handled as described in the section above.

## Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the services used by Napkin Notes or the functionality of the Android application change.

Any changes will be published on this page.
