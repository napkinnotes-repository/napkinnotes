# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: September 2026**

Napkin Notes is a scientific outreach publication available at [napkinnotes.es](https://napkinnotes.es) and through the Napkin Notes Android application.

The Android application displays content from napkinnotes.es using an Android WebView.

## Data collection

Napkin Notes does not require users to create an account and does not request access to contacts, precise location, camera, microphone, files, SMS, phone data, or other sensitive device permissions.

Napkin Notes uses **Cloudflare Web Analytics** to obtain aggregated usage statistics such as:

- page views,
- visits,
- country,
- browser,
- device type,
- operating system.

Cloudflare Web Analytics is designed as a privacy-focused analytics service and does not use cookies for this purpose.

The analytics information is used only to understand general traffic patterns and improve the website and application.

## External links

Napkin Notes articles may contain links to external websites, scientific publications, repositories, institutions, or other third-party services.

These external websites operate independently and may have their own privacy policies and data-processing practices. Napkin Notes is not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party websites.

## Personal information

Napkin Notes does not require users to provide personal information in order to read articles or use the Android application.

If users voluntarily contact Napkin Notes by email or through any future contact form, the information they provide will be used only to respond to their request or communication.

## Changes to this Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if the services used by Napkin Notes or the functionality of the Android application change.

Any changes will be published on this page.
